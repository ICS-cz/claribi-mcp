# Security Policy

Report vulnerabilities affecting the clariBI MCP server (the hosted
endpoint at `https://claribi.com/mcp/v1/`) or the published SDKs
(`claribi-mcp` on PyPI, `@claribicom/mcp` on npm) to
`security@claribi.com`.

Repository-wide policy lives at
<https://github.com/ICS-cz/Claribi/blob/master/SECURITY.md>.

## Scope

- `https://claribi.com/mcp/v1/` and its OAuth discovery endpoints
  under `/.well-known/`.
- `claribi-mcp` on PyPI, `@claribicom/mcp` on npm.

## What to include

- Affected component and version (where applicable).
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof-of-concept payload.
- The version (release tag or commit SHA) where the issue was
  observed.

## Response targets

- Acknowledgement: within 2 business days.
- Triage and severity: within 5 business days.
- Fix or mitigation for critical issues: within 7 days of
  confirmation.
